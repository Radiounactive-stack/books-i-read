My Reading List

Americanah

★★★★☆ 4.3/5 Amazon

An in-depth examination of China's ambitious industrial strategy and its race to dominate the technologies of the future, from semiconductors to electric vehicles.

★★★★☆ 4.5/5 Amazon

The thrilling true story of Anom, an encrypted phone company secretly run by the FBI, and how it ensnared thousands of criminals worldwide in one of history's largest sting operations.

★★★★★ 4.6/5 Amazon

The timeless epic fantasy following Frodo Baggins and the Fellowship on their perilous quest to destroy the One Ring and save Middle-earth from the Dark Lord Sauron.
